Summary:
The life of a lady cursed into an ugly cute cat that is loved has been adapted into a comic. "I'll have you turn into a cat, sister." Marie, a Viscount's young daughter, is cursed after her sister steals her fiancé away from her. Her reflection in the mirror is - an ugly cat! Marie runs for her life and collapses from loneliness and hunger. A kind hand is extended to her. "In the middle of this rain, poor thing...Would you like to come with me?" He is a person rumored to be the 'ruthless, merciless, undefeated duke.' This is the start of a happy cat life where an unfortunate girl finds her one and only happiness! An adventure fantasy with both comedy and romance about Marie, a viscount's daughter who has become an ugly cat! Comic book project in progress!
