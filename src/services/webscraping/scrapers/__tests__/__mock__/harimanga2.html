Read manhwa Princess Shu / Valia, Tribute to Shuden / Princess Shuden / Fairy Princess / La Princesa Hada / 꿀이흐르는 – 슈공녀 (스포有) / 슈공녀

Valia was born into a fallen noble family as the daughter of a knight and lived a sad life. Then, the opportunity of a second life comes to her.

‘Should I live like that again?’

No, she won’t!

Valia decides to become a temple princess in order to live a happier life without having to worry about money. But then, as a princess, she becomes the wife of Shuden, the most handsome man and highest-ranking noble in the Empire…!

But in the future, Shuden falls in love with a new woman from another world. Valia knows this, but she can’t get away from Shuden, who is in love with her!

Valia, Shuden’s princess–what is her fate?

“You are the only one in my life that I will love. If I break my oath…I will pay for it with my life.”

  2. EarthPotato

    I’ve only read until chapter 96, so my comments are limited.
    For me, this one is an S tier.
    The translation is OK, typical mistake on either he or she and you or me and some slight minor error, nothing big of a deal.
    The art style is great, but later on there’s some slight change into more basic style, but still, it’s not a problem for me as I get used to it.
    The story is good, it’s a little bit annoying due to the villains, but it’s not stress to read, the characters are so good, FL is good, the ML is good, the saint is good, the emperor is good, the villains are annoying but good and other supporting characters are good.
    The reason why I gave it a high rank is due to the relationship going on between the FL and ML, they are pretty much on honeymoon period for the whole time and fucking like rabbits on every chapters that make me jelly.
    They also good on handling the villains, they’re pretty much impenetrable when the villains try to poke them, so you don’t see any of that misunderstanding drama bullshit, but don’t get me wrong, there are still time when the close call situation happened too.
    I would recommend this series to other reader.
